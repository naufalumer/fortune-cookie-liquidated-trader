One fortune cookie line, at most 20 words, for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated.

> Your lucky numbers are 0, 0, and 0: the market kept the change.

The line opens in the classic fortune-cookie voice ("Your lucky numbers are..."), names the loss in the zeros of an emptied balance, and lands the joke on a double meaning: "kept the change" is both the market pocketing every last coin and the cashier's line for a tip the trader never meant to leave. Rueful, warm, and laughing with the trader, not at him. `main.py` writes exactly that line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
